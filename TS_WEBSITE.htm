From the team

We are students who design, build and race a Formula Student car. Multidisciplinary minds working as one crew, from the first sketch to the last lap of race weekend.

Team Stallions is the official Motorsports team of Nirma University, driven by a passion for innovation, engineering excellence and motorsport.

Departments

Mechanical, electrical, electronics and statics — every department works side by side to build, race and present the car.

Mechanical

We design, analyse and fabricate the parts that carry the driver and the loads: the structures, vehicle dynamics and drivetrain.

  • Structures
  • Suspension
  • Brakes & Wheels
  • Steering
  • Drivetrain

Electrical

Power, wiring and safety. We move energy where the car needs it and shut it off the moment it must be shut off.

  • Accumulator System
  • Motor & MCU

Electronics

Sensors, control units and data logging. The car's nervous system, and our window into what it is really doing on track.

  • Control Electronics
  • Embedded Systems, DAQ and Telemetry

Statics

Business plans, cost reports and design presentations. We prove the car on paper before it hits the track.

  • Engineering Design
  • Cost & Manufacturing
  • Business Plan & Sales
  • CAE
  • Sustainability
  • Mathworks Simulations
  • Innovation
  • Validation
  • Media Team
  • Sponsorship & Marketing
